# PesaPap Growth Analytics — Growth Memo

## Result

Group B achieved a conversion rate of 13.81%, compared with 10.46% for Group A. This represents a 3.34 percentage-point absolute lift and a 31.96% relative lift. The difference was statistically significant (chi-square = 7.8197, p = 0.0052). The 95% confidence interval for the conversion-rate difference was 1.01 to 5.67 percentage points.

## Impact

Assuming 1,000 users per month and similar conversion rates, Group B could generate approximately 138 conversions compared with 105 for Group A — around 33 additional conversions per month.

## Recommendation

Proceed with a controlled rollout of Group B. Monitor conversion rates, customer experience, and business outcomes before expanding the change to all users.

## Caveats

The estimated impact assumes that the experiment results generalize to future users. Actual outcomes may differ. Continue monitoring performance after rollout and investigate other business metrics before making a permanent decision.
